
What to do if items are missing or incorrect in your order 
3 months ago Updated 
If you have received your Morena Mia order but notice some items are missing or incorrect, this guide will help you resolve the issue efficiently and ensure you receive the correct products promptly. 
Step 1: Verify Your Order 
Carefully check your receipt or order confirmation and compare it with the items you received. Make a note of any missing items, wrong products, or discrepancies. 
Step 2: Document the Issue 
Take clear photos or videos showing the items you received and highlight any missing or incorrect products. This documentation will help our support team better understand the problem. 
Step 3: Contact Morena Mia Customer Service 
Reach out to our dedicated customer support team via email 
(customerservice@emacaribbean.com), phone (+1 888 249 4189), or WhatsApp (+1 555 858 1145). Please provide your order number, along with a description of the issue and any relevant photos or videos you have taken. 
Step 4: Resolution Process 
Our team will promptly investigate the issue you reported. Depending on the situation, the resolution may include sending replacement items or providing a return label for incorrect products. 
  
Additional Support and Information
For further assistance, tracking updates, or questions regarding your order, our dedicated customer support team is available through the following official channels:
- Email: customerservice@emacaribbean.com
- Toll-Free Phone: +1 888 249 4189
- Business Hours: Monday through Friday, 8:00 AM – 5:00 PM EST

Please have your order confirmation or invoice number ready when contacting support to expedite your request.
