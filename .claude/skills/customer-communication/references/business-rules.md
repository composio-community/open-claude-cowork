## 4. How the signed terms and the real protocol fit together

The ticket says sales are final, so the written position stays that way and a message never *opens*
with a refund. But the protocol does have a refund path, and once it applies it is good news that can
be stated plainly. Work down this ladder:

1. **Resolve first.** Offer compensation from the very first contact; do not wait for the customer to
   demand it. Pick what fits the case (replacement, complementary product, GBK gift, discount).
   What can be offered and at what value: `compensation-catalog.md`.
2. **Compensation rejected + purchase under $500 USD + no open dispute → refund approved.** Explain the
   method for their country: `refunds-and-disputes.md`.
3. **$500 USD or more → escalate.** Do not promise. Say the case is under review and when they will hear back.
4. **Open dispute with the bank → refund on hold** until the customer cancels it.
5. **Defective, missing or wrong product → replacement is always authorized**, any amount, no escalation.

| Situation | Rule |
|---|---|
| Old purchase (e.g. last year) | No full refund. Apologise for the delay, review pending shipments, escalate for review. |
| Used product, no results | No full refund. Validate the frustration, explain results vary, escalate as a possible exception. |
| Signed policy invoked | No refund. Offer in-protocol options, escalate. |
| Medical / delicate case (doctor's instruction, skin problem) | Exception only, must escalate. Safety first, ask for skin-condition details, share LED therapy information. |
| VIP ($20K+) | Listen carefully, verify documentation, escalate to the store manager, internal investigation. |
| Promotional prices | Must be honored. |
| Device warranty | Lifetime warranty, replacement only, never a refund. |
| Customer asks to cancel a shipment | If it was already processed it cannot be cancelled: the terms are in the shipping form the customer agreed to. Say so clearly and professionally, then offer what is possible (tracking, address confirmation). Because it is a "no", deliver it live on the call. |

When a refund does not apply: apologise, review, present the in-protocol options, never promise a full
refund, and escalate rather than refuse flatly.

## 5. Escalation triggers (feed the internal escalation note)

Escalate, and add the internal note after the block, when any of these is true:

- The remedy is outside policy or an exception (medical, delicate client).
- Purchase is $500 USD or more and the customer rejected the compensation.
- The customer cites legal action, BBB, PROFECO, a bank dispute/chargeback or public reviews.
- VIP ($20K+) account.
- Repeat offender or prior disputes on the same customer.
- Safety, privacy or data-protection element.
- The request contradicts a written commitment already made.

## 6. Reframing flow

**Listen → Understand → Build trust → Reframe → Collaborate → Solve.**

1. Identify and validate the real concern behind the frustration; confirm it with empathy.
2. Move the conversation from confrontation to collaboration.
3. State policies and limits with clarity, respect and empathy.
4. Offer value solutions within the protocol.
5. Guide toward a concrete solution instead of only reacting to the request.

Success signal: *"I'm still not happy, but I'm willing to work with you."*

## 7. Scripts by situation

| Situation | Approach |
|---|---|
| Very upset, demands full refund, threatens review or dispute | Empathetic apology, validate that they felt pressured, review the case and the options; open with a compensation offer. |
| Demands refund, claims a 30-day guarantee was promised, used product with no results | Validate, clarify results vary, do not offer a full refund, escalate for an exception. |
| Immediate cancellation + refund, legal threat, "was pressured before a flight" | Review the case, clarify charges with documentation, escalate if it doesn't apply; mention the dispute explanation only if they refuse to work with us. |
| Legal threat after a doctor's instruction | Safety first, ask for skin-condition details, share LED therapy information, limited exceptions, escalate. |
| Demands management (repeat offender, prior disputes) | Review the earlier internal investigation, reship by express at no cost, re-escalate. |
| VIP ($20K+) with regret | Careful listening and review, verify documentation, escalate to the store manager. |
| No refund available (last year's purchase) | Apologise for the delay, review pending shipments, explain a full refund does not apply, escalate. |
| Upset by incorrect information; threatens BBB/PROFECO and reviews; open disputes | Compare what was communicated with what is on file, warn about imitations (Temu/eBay) that carry no warranty, escalate and offer a fair resolution. |
