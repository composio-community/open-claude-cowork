# Product Issues, Replacements and Shipping

For defective, missing or wrong products, replacements, return labels and shipping questions.
A replacement for a defective, missing or wrong product is **always authorized**, at any amount, with no
escalation. Email plus a voicemail to confirm the shipping address is the normal first contact.

## 1. Defective device or product (ATOX, cream, device)

1. Ask for a short video (30 seconds to 1 minute) that shows and explains the malfunction.
2. The technical team verifies the fault.
3. Stock is checked before anything is promised. If the model is discontinued, offer an equivalent or newer
   model, subject to availability (PENDING #5: never name a specific device until stock is confirmed).
   Examples of past equivalents: Marvelous Signature Pro for Marvelous Gold; Gorilla Device for Jelessi;
   Ultimate Infra-red LED Face and Body as a modern upgrade.
4. If the defective item must come back, a FedEx return label is sent to the customer.
5. Replacement ships overnight. Processing of a returned defective item takes about 14 days; in urgent
   cases the replacement can start once the customer has dropped the package at FedEx.

Ask the customer to: check items against the receipt, take clear photos or videos of the packaging and
products, and notify us right away by email or WhatsApp.

## 2. Missing or wrong product

For a device that does not work, was lost, or is missing, also consult `assets/missing products.txt` and follow it where it is more specific than this file.

1. Confirm the product was on the invoice and was meant to ship (or to be handed over in store).
2. Confirm in shipping records whether it was actually sent.
3. Ask for photos of everything received, and compare them with the invoice.
4. If confirmed missing or wrong, ship the replacement overnight.

Ask the customer for: order number, description of what is missing or wrong, and photo or video evidence.
Contact channels for them: email, phone or WhatsApp.

## 3. Shipping

- Always confirm the address. **No PO boxes and no mail-delivery facilities.**
- Overnight shipping for replacements; give the customer the FedEx tracking link when there is one.
- Carrier questions: tell the customer FedEx may need additional information from them, or that the
  package is waiting at a FedEx branch (PENDING #6 on agent follow-up for shipping cases).
- Cancelling a shipment that was already processed is not possible; see `business-rules.md` §4.
- Delivery confirmation from FedEx alone does not close a case: we wait for the customer to confirm they
  received the product, the instructions or the refund.

## 4. Warranty and pricing reminders

- Devices carry a lifetime warranty: replacement only, never a refund.
- Promotional prices must be honored.
- Cosmetics cannot be returned for health and safety reasons.

## 5. Product-usage instructions

When the case is "how do I use this product", send the instructions and follow up until the customer
confirms they received them. The product-instructions document is in `assets/PRODUCT INSTRUCTIONS.txt`;
read it only for these cases.
